THE DETAILS BEHIND THE DECISION

A little guidance. A clearer review.

How Proof helps you check labels, and where your judgment comes in.

TTB labeling resources
01

Start with the application.

Upload a clear, readable image with all required label panels visible. Enter the expected brand, beverage type, class, ABV, net contents, producer, and origin for imports.

For a batch, use the CSV template and match each row to its image filename. Up to 300 images are processed with two OCR workers.

Download batch template
02

Let the details line up.

Brand and class comparisons allow case, spacing, and smart apostrophe differences. ABV values must agree, and proof is cross-checked. Net contents supports equivalent milliliters, liters, and US fluid ounces.

Low OCR confidence and ambiguous text stay open for review. Red flags identify missing information or mismatches, rather than making the final decision.

03

Give the warning a closer look.

The required statement is compared word for word, allowing line wrapping. The header must be uppercase. Inspect the artwork to confirm a bold header, regular-weight body, readable type and contrast.

Private by design.
